Taptale Defender

Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Taptale Defender ("the Game"), developed and published by Rubén Carreño.

Taptale Defender is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide game functionality, synchronize game progress, maintain leaderboards, authenticate players and protect the Game and its online services against abuse.

Taptale Defender does not sell your personal information and does not use your game data for targeted advertising.

2. Information Processed by the Game

Depending on the features you use, Taptale Defender may process limited information necessary for the operation of the Game.

This may include:

  • Game progress and save data.
  • Player level, statistics, achievements and related gameplay information.
  • Leaderboard scores.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • A technical account identifier used for authentication.
  • Technical information required to verify legitimate access to online game services.

The Game does not require you to provide your real name, postal address, phone number or other similar personal information directly to the developer.

3. Google Play Games Services

Taptale Defender integrates Google Play Games Services.

Google Play Games may be used for features such as:

  • Player authentication.
  • Leaderboards.
  • Game-related player identification.
  • Synchronization of supported game services.

When you use Google Play Games Services, Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and account settings.

Taptale Defender does not receive your Google account password.

4. Firebase Authentication

Taptale Defender uses Firebase Authentication to securely associate game data with a player account.

Authentication may use your Google Play Games identity to create or identify a Firebase user.

The Game may store an internal Firebase user identifier associated with your game progress. This identifier is used for technical purposes and does not require the developer to know your real-world identity.

5. Cloud Save and Game Data

Taptale Defender includes cloud synchronization features designed to preserve and synchronize game progress.

Cloud save information may include:

  • Character and progression data.
  • Player level and experience.
  • Inventory and game resources.
  • Unlocks and progression milestones.
  • Gameplay configuration required to restore your save.

Cloud game information may be processed using Google Firebase services, including Firestore and Cloud Functions.

Save data may also be maintained locally on your device to allow the Game to continue functioning when cloud services are temporarily unavailable.

6. Local Data

Taptale Defender stores certain information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Local save data.
  • Game preferences.
  • Interface settings.
  • Temporary synchronization information.

Some locally stored data may be removed when you clear the application's data or uninstall the Game.

7. Leaderboards

Taptale Defender may submit gameplay scores, such as player level or other eligible statistics, to Google Play Games leaderboards.

Depending on your Google Play Games privacy settings, your player name, profile image or score may be visible to other players through Google Play Games.

Taptale Defender may also perform server-side verification of leaderboard information to reduce cheating, manipulation and fraudulent score submissions.

8. Firebase and Google Cloud Services

Taptale Defender uses services provided by Google and Firebase for technical game functionality.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore.
  • Firebase Cloud Functions.
  • Firebase App Check.
  • Google Play Integrity.
  • Google Play Games Services.

These services may automatically process limited technical information required to provide, secure and protect their services, including device, application or network-related information.

Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

9. Security and Fraud Prevention

Taptale Defender uses technical security systems designed to protect game services and reduce unauthorized access, cheating, automated abuse and manipulation of game data.

These systems may include Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity.

These services may evaluate technical information about the application, device and installation in order to determine whether requests originate from a legitimate instance of the Game.

10. Purchases

If Taptale Defender offers digital purchases, transactions are processed by Google Play.

The developer does not receive or store your complete payment card or banking information.

Google Play may provide the Game with technical purchase information, such as a purchase identifier, product identifier or purchase token, where necessary to verify purchases and grant purchased content.

11. Advertising

Taptale Defender does not currently use third-party advertising networks or targeted advertising systems.

12. Analytics

Taptale Defender does not currently use third-party analytics services for behavioral advertising or user profiling.

Technical service providers may nevertheless process limited diagnostic or operational information as necessary to provide their underlying services.

13. Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell or rent player information.

Information may be processed by service providers strictly where necessary to operate the Game, including Google and Firebase services used for authentication, cloud storage, security, leaderboards and related functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security or integrity of the Game and its users.

14. Data Retention

Game-related cloud data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide synchronization, account functionality, security and game progression services.

Local data remains on your device until it is overwritten, deleted, application data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled.

Certain records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, transaction verification or legal compliance.
